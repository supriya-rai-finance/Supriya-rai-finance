06 — ORDER CONVERSION

The key research question: order book → revenue → cash

For an EPC-heavy business, headline order-book growth needs to be assessed alongside execution capability, project margins, working capital and cash conversion.

01

Order Intake

Monitor size, sector, geography, customer concentration and order quality.

02

Execution

Track revenue conversion, project progress, margins and execution milestones.

03

Cash Conversion

Analyse receivables, advances, working capital and operating cash flow against accounting profit.

Analytical framework: A growing order book is most useful when accompanied by healthy project execution, sustainable margins and improving cash conversion.

09 — VALUATION FRAMEWORK

Multi-method valuation framework

Because L&T combines EPC, manufacturing, technology services, financial services and other businesses, a single valuation method can obscure differences in capital intensity and earnings quality.

DCF

Discounted Cash Flow

Enterprise Value = Σ FCF / (1 + WACC)^t + Terminal Value

Key assumptions: revenue growth, operating margin, working capital, capex, WACC and terminal growth.

RELATIVE VALUATION

P/E and EV/EBITDA

Equity Value = EPS × Selected P/E

Peer selection should account for business mix, cyclicality, leverage, international exposure and earnings quality.

SOTP

Sum-of-the-Parts

SOTP = EPC + Energy + Hi-Tech + IT/TS + Financial Services

A segment-based approach can help separate mature cash generators from higher-growth or separately valued businesses.

CROSS-CHECK

Book Value / ROE

P/B = Market Price / Book Value

Useful as a cross-check alongside return on equity and sustainable earnings growth.

Important: This portfolio page intentionally does not publish a fabricated target price. A live valuation should be populated using a clearly dated share price, explicit forecast assumptions and documented peer inputs.

14 — RESEARCH CONCLUSION

Analytical conclusion

L&T's FY2025–26 results provide a useful case study in analysing a diversified engineering and infrastructure group. The combination of a ₹7.40 lakh crore order book, ₹2.86 lakh crore consolidated revenue and ₹17,238 crore recurring PAT creates a framework for studying the relationship between order visibility, project execution and profitability.


The next layer of analysis should focus on conversion of the order book into revenue and cash, segment-level profitability, working-capital discipline, international exposure and the valuation assigned to different business segments.


For an equity-research model, the principal objective is not simply to identify revenue growth, but to determine how much of that growth converts into sustainable earnings, cash flow and incremental return on capital.
