02 — COMPANY OVERVIEW

Diversified engineering, infrastructure and technology platform

L&T operates across infrastructure, energy, manufacturing, technology services, financial services, realty and other businesses. From FY2026–27, the Group changed its reporting structure under its Lakshya 2031 strategic plan.

12 — VALUATION FRAMEWORK

Multi-method valuation approach

L&T's diversified business model means that valuation can be approached through several complementary methods rather than relying on a single multiple.

METHOD 01

Discounted Cash Flow

EV = Σ FCF / (1 + WACC)^t + Terminal Value

Key assumptions: revenue growth, EBITDA margin, tax rate, capex, working capital, WACC and terminal growth.

METHOD 02

P/E

Equity Value = EPS × Selected P/E

Peer selection should reflect business mix, growth, return ratios, leverage and earnings quality.

METHOD 03

EV / EBITDA

EV = EBITDA × Selected EV / EBITDA

Useful for comparing capital-intensive operating businesses with different financing structures.

METHOD 04

Sum-of-the-Parts

SOTP = Core EPC + Energy + Technology + Financial Services + Realty

A segment-based approach can distinguish businesses with different growth, capital intensity and valuation profiles.

No fabricated target price: This portfolio report deliberately does not publish an unsupported target price. A live target-price model should use a clearly dated market price, explicit forecast assumptions, peer multiples and a documented sensitivity analysis.

17 — RESEARCH CONCLUSION

Analytical conclusion

L&T provides a useful equity-research case study because its financial profile combines a large engineering and infrastructure order book with energy, manufacturing, technology services, financial services and realty businesses.


FY2026 ended with a ₹7.40 lakh crore order book, ₹2.86 lakh crore consolidated revenue and ₹17,238 crore recurring profit attributable to owners. The June 2026 order book increased further to ₹7.79 lakh crore, while Q1 FY2027 order inflows increased 14% year on year.


The principal research question going forward is the quality of conversion: how effectively L&T converts its order visibility into revenue, sustainable margins, operating cash flow and incremental returns on capital.


The valuation framework should therefore combine earnings multiples, cash-flow analysis and a sum-of-the-parts approach, with explicit sensitivity to execution, working capital, international exposure and segment economics.
