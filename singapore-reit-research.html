01 — SECTOR OVERVIEW

Singapore REIT investment framework

REIT analysis requires a different framework from conventional operating-company equity research. Distribution sustainability, property values, occupancy, lease structure, leverage and funding costs are central to the analysis.

Income

Distribution Profile

Analyse distributable income, DPU growth, payout sustainability, distribution yield and the quality of recurring rental income.

Property

Asset Quality

Assess occupancy, rental reversions, tenant concentration, WALE, location, asset type and portfolio diversification.

Capital

Balance Sheet

Monitor aggregate leverage, interest coverage, debt maturity, fixed-rate debt, cost of debt and refinancing requirements.

04 — OPERATING PERFORMANCE

Revenue, occupancy and rental growth

Income

Net Property Income

NPI growth should be decomposed into rental reversions, occupancy changes, acquisitions, divestments, asset enhancement initiatives and property operating expenses.

Leasing

Occupancy & WALE

High occupancy can support income visibility, while WALE and lease expiry concentration provide insight into future leasing risk and potential rental resets.

NPI Growth ≈ Rental Growth + Occupancy Change + Portfolio Growth + Asset Enhancement Effects
05 — DISTRIBUTION ANALYSIS

DPU and income sustainability

Distribution per Unit is one of the key earnings measures for REIT investors, but the quality and sustainability of the underlying distributable income also matter.

DPU

Distribution Growth

Track DPU over multiple reporting periods and separate organic rental growth from acquisitions, disposals and capital-market activity.

Income

Distributable Income

Analyse the bridge from property income to distributable income and identify recurring versus non-recurring items.

Yield

Distribution Yield

Compare distribution yield with relevant REIT peers, Singapore government bond yields and the broader cost of capital environment.

06 — BALANCE SHEET

Gearing and financing risk

07 — INTEREST RATE SENSITIVITY

Interest rates and REIT valuations

Higher-rate environment

Higher borrowing costs can reduce distributable income and increase refinancing expenses. Higher risk-free yields can also change the relative attractiveness of REIT distributions.

Lower-rate environment

Lower funding costs can support distribution growth and improve valuation support, although property fundamentals and market expectations remain important.

Estimated DPU Impact ≈ Change in Interest Expense ÷ Units Outstanding

09 — VALUATION

REIT valuation framework

REIT valuation combines income-based and asset-based approaches. No single multiple should be interpreted independently from property quality, financing structure and interest rates.

Income

Dividend / DPU Model

A DPU-based model can estimate equity value by discounting expected distributions and applying an appropriate terminal growth assumption.

Value = DPU₁ / (Ke − g)
Asset

Price / NAV

Compare the market price with adjusted net asset value and investigate the reasons for any premium or discount.

P/NAV = Market Price / NAV per Unit
Income

Distribution Yield

Compare forward DPU yield with peers and relevant Singapore government bond yields.

Yield = Forward DPU / Unit Price
10 — ILLUSTRATIVE VALUATION

Scenario-based valuation framework

The following framework is intentionally illustrative. It is designed to demonstrate how an analyst can structure valuation sensitivity rather than present a live target price.

DPU sensitivity

A research model can test different combinations of forward DPU, required return and long-term growth.

Bear Lower DPU growth · Higher required return
Base Moderate DPU growth · Normalised funding environment
Bull Higher DPU growth · Lower funding pressure
Illustrative Value = Forward DPU / (Required Return − Growth)
11 — NAV ANALYSIS

Net asset value framework

NAV provides an asset-based valuation perspective. The key analytical question is whether property valuations adequately reflect rental income, capitalisation rates and current market conditions.

Property Valuation

Review independent property valuations, capitalisation rates, comparable transactions and changes in fair value.

NAV Sensitivity

Stress-test NAV for changes in property values and assess how leverage affects NAV per unit.

NAV per Unit = (Net Property Assets − Liabilities) / Units Outstanding
12 — FOOTBALL FIELD

Valuation triangulation

A professional REIT valuation can present several independent frameworks side by side rather than depending on a single valuation method.

Method 1

DPU / DDM

Distribution-based valuation under bear, base and bull assumptions.

Method 2

P/NAV

Market price relative to adjusted net asset value and peer historical ranges.

Method 3

Distribution Yield

Forward yield comparison against peers and the interest-rate environment.

The football-field framework is presented as an analytical methodology. No live target price is assigned on this page.
13 — CATALYSTS & RISKS

Key research variables

Potential Catalysts

Rental reversions, occupancy improvement, asset enhancement initiatives, acquisitions, capital recycling, lower funding costs and stronger property valuations.

Key Risks

Higher interest rates, refinancing costs, falling property valuations, tenant weakness, vacancy, dilution from equity raising and weaker rental demand.

Macro Variables

Singapore interest rates, government bond yields, economic growth, office demand, retail spending and property-market conditions.

Balance Sheet Variables

Leverage, debt maturity, fixed-rate debt, interest coverage, cost of debt and available liquidity.

14 — ANALYST FRAMEWORK

How I would analyse a Singapore REIT

Step 01

Understand the portfolio, asset classes, geography and tenant concentration.

Step 02

Analyse occupancy, rental reversions, WALE and leasing pipeline.

Step 03

Build a DPU and distributable-income forecast.

Step 04

Stress-test leverage, refinancing and interest-rate assumptions.

Step 05

Calculate NAV and compare market price with NAV.

Step 06

Compare valuation with relevant Singapore REIT peers.

15 — RESEARCH CONCLUSION

Key takeaways

Singapore REIT analysis requires a combination of property-level research, income forecasting, balance-sheet analysis and valuation. CICT provides a useful case study because its diversified commercial portfolio allows analysis across retail, office, occupancy, rental income, leverage and capital management.


The research process should ultimately connect three areas: property fundamentals → distributable income → valuation.


For portfolio construction or investment decisions, the model should be refreshed with the latest unit price, DPU guidance, interest rates, property valuations and company disclosures.
