02 · FUNDAMENTAL FRAMEWORK

Justified P/B from bank economics

P/B=(ROE − g)÷(Ke − g)

Sustainable growth is modelled as ROE × (1 − payout). Cost of equity is CAPM: risk-free rate + beta × equity risk premium. The workbook applies this framework independently to each bank.
