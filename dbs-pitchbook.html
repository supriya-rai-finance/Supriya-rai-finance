07 · VALUATION SUMMARY

Valuation framework

For banks, the framework links profitability, book value, earnings and capital returns. The current DBS model uses P/B as the primary sensitivity and supplements it with P/E and dividend-yield lenses.

P/B

FY2025 net book value: S$24.29/share. Illustrative 2.4x–3.6x sensitivity gives S$58.30–S$87.44.

P/E

Illustrative 16x–20x range applied to FY2025 basic EPS of S$3.88 gives S$62.08–S$77.60.

Dividend Yield

Illustrative 4.0%–5.0% yield applied to FY2025 DPS of S$3.06 gives S$61.20–S$76.50.

Important: These are sensitivity assumptions for portfolio demonstration. They are not analyst targets, forecasts or investment recommendations.

09 · CATALYSTS & RISKS

Variables to monitor

Potential earnings catalysts

  • Wealth-management activity and net new money
  • Fee-income growth and treasury customer sales
  • Loan and deposit growth
  • Operating efficiency
  • Capital deployment and dividend capacity

Key risks / sensitivities

  • NIM compression as rates and deposit pricing change
  • Credit deterioration and higher provisions
  • Regional macroeconomic conditions
  • Capital or regulatory changes
  • Market volatility affecting wealth and trading activity
The research framework treats these as monitoring variables rather than predetermined outcomes. Quarterly results should be used to update the thesis and valuation assumptions.
