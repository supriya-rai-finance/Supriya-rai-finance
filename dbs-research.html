01 · Investment Thesis

How I assess DBS

The thesis is organised around the few drivers that matter most for a large bank: franchise quality, earnings durability, capital strength, valuation and risk.

01

Business Quality

Assess Singapore franchise strength, regional presence, wealth management, institutional relationships and digital capabilities.

02

Earnings Drivers

Track NII, NIM, fee income, wealth-management activity, loan growth, treasury customer sales and operating efficiency.

03

Valuation

Use P/B, P/E, dividend capacity and sustainable ROE together rather than relying on a single multiple.

04

Capital & Asset Quality

Monitor CET1, NPLs, credit costs, liquidity and balance-sheet growth as constraints on future returns.

05

Catalysts

Watch fee-income growth, wealth flows, loan growth, efficiency and capital deployment.

06

Risks

Monitor NIM compression, credit deterioration, market volatility, regulation and regional macro conditions.

02 · Company Overview

Business model & franchise

DBS operates across consumer banking, wealth management and institutional banking, with technology and digital capabilities supporting the franchise.

CONSUMER & WEALTH

Consumer Banking / Wealth Management

Deposits, lending, cards, investments and wealth-management services. Wealth management is an important fee-income contributor.

INSTITUTIONAL

Institutional Banking

Corporate and institutional relationships across transaction banking, financing, advisory and capital-markets activities.

DIGITAL

Technology & AI

Digital banking and technology-led operating capabilities support customer experience and operating efficiency.

05 · Valuation Framework

Bank valuation logic

The valuation section is intentionally consolidated: the dashboard above contains the live sensitivity and football field, while this section explains the methodology rather than repeating the same numbers.

P/B

Book Value + ROE

Compare price-to-book with sustainable ROE and the bank's cost of equity. Book value is a central anchor for bank valuation.

P/E

Earnings Multiple

Use forward or normalised earnings to test whether the market multiple is consistent with expected profitability.

DIVIDEND

Distribution Capacity

Assess dividend yield and payout sustainability alongside capital requirements and earnings generation.

Model note: The dashboard's numerical sensitivities are illustrative portfolio-analysis assumptions. They are not analyst targets, forecasts or recommendations.
06 · Catalysts & Risks

Variables to monitor

Potential earnings catalysts

  • Wealth-management flows and fee growth
  • Loan and deposit growth
  • Operating efficiency
  • Capital deployment and distribution capacity
  • Growth in customer-related treasury activity

Key risks / sensitivities

  • NIM compression from rate and funding changes
  • Higher credit costs or asset-quality deterioration
  • Regional macroeconomic weakness
  • Market volatility affecting wealth and trading activity
  • Changes in regulatory capital requirements
