07 · VALUATION FRAMEWORK

Multi-method approach

DCF

FCFF discounted using WACC with explicit assumptions for revenue growth, margins, capex and working capital.

P/E

Equity-value cross-check using EPS and selected peer / historical multiples.

EV / EBITDA

Useful for comparing operating businesses with different financing structures.

SOTP

Separates businesses with different growth, cyclicality, capital intensity and valuation characteristics.

Valuation discipline: the research page does not publish an unsupported target price. A live valuation should be refreshed with dated market inputs, explicit forecasts, peer multiples and sensitivity analysis.

08 · CATALYSTS & RISKS

Variables to monitor

Potential catalysts

  • Sustained order inflows
  • Faster backlog conversion
  • Working-capital discipline
  • Energy transition awards
  • Technology / data-centre scaling

Key risks

  • Project delays and cost escalation
  • Middle East execution concentration
  • Margin pressure from project mix
  • Working-capital reversal
  • International / forex exposure
These are monitoring variables, not predetermined outcomes. Quarterly disclosures should be used to update the research model.
