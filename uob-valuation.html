ANALYST VIEW

What the model tests

P/B framework

For a bank, the case focuses on equity-based valuation lenses rather than an industrial FCFF framework. The workbook tests P/B, P/E and dividend-yield reference ranges alongside reported profitability, NIM, asset quality and capital.

Scenario & sensitivity discipline

Outputs are treated as model evidence rather than a standalone conclusion. Assumptions should be refreshed against current filings and market data before external use.
