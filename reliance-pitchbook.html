03 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Reliance is a diversified earnings and capital-allocation story

FY2026 establishes the financial base; Q1 FY2027 provides the latest reported operating update.

Consumer engines

Digital Services and Retail are important growth businesses and together contributed more than 55% of consolidated EBITDA in FY2026 according to Reliance's analyst presentation.

Energy platform

O2C and Oil & Gas remain important earnings contributors with exposure to commodity prices, spreads, production and global demand.

Capital allocation

FY2026 net debt was ₹1,24,717 Cr and net debt / EBITDA was 0.60x; capex was ₹1,44,271 Cr.

Q1 FY2027 reported gross revenue of ₹3,40,257 Cr, EBITDA of ₹54,067 Cr, PAT of ₹23,196 Cr and capex of ₹38,682 Cr.

04 · COMPANY OVERVIEW

Six analytical business engines

Jio / Digital Services

Track subscribers, ARPU, tariff changes, network investment, EBITDA growth and operating leverage.

Energy

Track production, gas prices, development expenditure and resource productivity.

New Energy

Media
