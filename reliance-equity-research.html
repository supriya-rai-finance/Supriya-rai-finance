01 — Company Overview

A diversified Indian conglomerate

Reliance Industries operates across multiple businesses including Digital Services, Retail, Oil-to-Chemicals, Oil & Gas and Media & Entertainment. The diversified structure means consolidated earnings should be analysed alongside segment-level economics.

Core analytical question

How much of Reliance's earnings and valuation is supported by established cash-generating businesses versus businesses with higher future growth expectations?

Valuation implication

A combination of DCF, trading multiples and Sum-of-the-Parts analysis can provide a more complete valuation framework than a single consolidated P/E multiple.

02 — Business Segment Analysis

Understanding the earnings engine

Digital Services

Jio provides telecommunications and digital services. Key variables include subscriber growth, ARPU, tariff changes, network investment and operating leverage.

Retail

Reliance Retail operates across grocery, consumer electronics, fashion and other retail categories. Key variables include store productivity, same-store growth, margins and digital commerce.

Oil-to-Chemicals

The O2C business remains exposed to crude prices, refining margins, petrochemical spreads, utilization and global demand.

Oil & Gas

Production growth, gas prices, development expenditure and resource productivity influence this segment.

New Energy

Battery manufacturing, solar manufacturing, hydrogen and associated investments represent longer-duration growth opportunities.

Media & Entertainment

Advertising, subscriptions, content economics and the integration of digital platforms are important variables.

09 — SOTP Valuation

Sum-of-the-Parts framework

A SOTP approach separates Reliance's major businesses and assigns business-specific valuation methodologies. This is particularly useful because Jio, Retail, O2C and Oil & Gas have different economics.

Segment Primary valuation method Key driver
Digital Services / Jio EV/EBITDA Subscriber growth, ARPU, EBITDA
Retail EV/EBITDA / P/E Revenue growth, margins, store productivity
O2C EV/EBITDA Refining margins, spreads, utilization
Oil & Gas EV/EBITDA Production and gas economics
New Energy Scenario valuation Capacity, commercialization and margins
Why SOTP matters: The market may assign different valuation multiples to mature cash-generating businesses and higher-growth businesses. SOTP therefore provides a useful cross-check against the consolidated DCF.

Key variables to monitor

Catalysts

Risks

  • Commodity-price volatility
