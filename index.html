03 — RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

How I approach equity research

My research process combines business understanding, financial statement analysis, earnings drivers, valuation and risk assessment to build a structured view of a company.

01

Industry & Business

Understand the business model, industry structure, competitive position, customers, geographic exposure and key operating drivers.

02

Financial Analysis

Analyse revenue, margins, profitability, cash flow, balance sheet strength, working capital and return metrics.

03

Earnings & Drivers

Track earnings growth, order books, segment performance, operating trends, management commentary and business catalysts.

04

Valuation

Apply DCF, P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B or SOTP depending on the company's business model, capital intensity and earnings profile.

05

Catalysts & Risks

Identify measurable operating catalysts and downside variables, then define the indicators that should be monitored over time.

06

Research Conclusion

Bring the evidence together into a structured research conclusion supported by financial data, valuation assumptions and clearly stated risks.

Research principle: Focus on evidence, transparent assumptions and measurable financial drivers rather than relying only on headline market narratives.
