07 · EARNINGS DRIVERS

What moves the P&L

Net interest income

Loan growth, deposit pricing, funding costs and benchmark rates drive NIM and NII.

Non-interest income

Fees, wealth management, trading and insurance can diversify earnings when NIM is under pressure.

Credit costs

NPL formation, provisions, collateral and macro conditions are key downside variables.

Monitoring framework: refresh NIM, loans, deposits, fee income, CIR, NPLs, credit costs and capital at each results cycle.

11 · VALUATION

Bank-specific valuation framework

P / B

Relate market value to book value while considering sustainable ROE and capital.

P / E

Compare earnings multiples alongside earnings quality and growth.

Dividend yield

Frame shareholder distributions relative to market price and payout capacity.

Model discipline: refresh market price, book value, earnings, dividends and assumptions before using valuation outputs.
